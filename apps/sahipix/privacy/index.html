Privacy Policy: SahiPix: Exam Form Photo & Doc Kit

Android app package: app.sahipix.formkit · Developer: Lesster

This policy explains what information the SahiPix: Exam Form Photo & Doc Kit app ("the app") handles, and how. In short: we do not collect your personal data on our servers. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, described below.

What the app does with your files

SahiPix lets you pick a photo, signature or document from your device and converts it to the size, dimensions and file format required by an exam application form. All of this processing happens on your phone. Your photos and documents are not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see them.

Files you export are saved only where you choose on your device. Any temporary copies the app makes while editing stay in the app's private storage on your device and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions

  • The app does not request camera, storage, location or contacts permission. You pick a file through the Android system picker, so the app only receives the specific file you select.
  • Internet: used only for the AdMob banner and Google Play Billing, not to send your files anywhere.
  • Advertising ID: requested so the free version can show a Google AdMob banner. Pro removes the banner.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner. The app requests your device's advertising ID so ads can be served and measured. Buying the Pro upgrade removes the banner. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade (product id: sahipix_pro, a one-time purchase of Rs 49 to 99) is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the app can unlock Pro features. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. Apart from Google AdMob (for the banner in the free version) and Google Play Billing (if you buy Pro), the app does not send your data to third parties. Your photos and documents stay on your device.

Security

Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent over the internet (such as ad requests and Google Play Billing) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is kept on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time from within the app where available, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of it on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still email us with any privacy request and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
