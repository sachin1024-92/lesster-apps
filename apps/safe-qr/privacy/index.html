Privacy Policy: Safe QR & UPI Scanner

Android app package: in.successtips.safeqr · Developer: Lesster

This policy explains what information the Safe QR & UPI Scanner app ("the app") handles, and how. In short: we do not collect your personal data on our servers. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, described below.

How scanning works

Safe QR uses your camera to read QR codes. The camera image is processed on your device only to decode the QR code; images are not saved or uploaded. The decoded content (for example a link or a UPI payment request) is checked on your device against known scam patterns, such as a "scan to receive money" request or a payee name that does not match.

We do not upload, store or sell the codes you scan. Any scan history is stored only on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The app never makes a payment by itself; if you choose to pay, it hands the request to the UPI app you pick.

Optional link check (Google Safe Browsing)

The Play version can optionally check a decoded link with Google's Safe Browsing service. This happens only if a Safe Browsing key is configured. If that check runs, only the link is sent to Google, not a photo of the QR code. That is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

A separate FOSS build of this app has no internet permission and shows no ads.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan QR codes. Used only while the scanner screen is open. You can also pick an image through the Android system picker if you choose to scan a QR code from a photo; the app does not request storage permission for this.
  • Internet: used for the AdMob banner and the UMP consent form, Google Play Billing, and any optional Safe Browsing link check (only if a key is configured).
  • Advertising ID: requested so the Play version can show a Google AdMob banner after consent.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free Play version shows a Google AdMob banner on the history screen, after the consent step described below. The app requests your device's advertising ID so ads can be served and measured. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Before ads are shown, the app presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, where required, so you can choose whether to allow personalised ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade (product id: safeqr_pro, a one-time purchase of Rs 49) is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the app can unlock Pro features. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. Apart from Google AdMob and the UMP consent form, Google Play Billing (if you buy Pro), and an optional Safe Browsing link check (only if a Safe Browsing key is configured), the app does not send the codes you scan to third parties.

Security

Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent over the internet (such as ad and consent requests, Google Play Billing, and any optional Safe Browsing checks) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is kept on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time from within the app where available, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of it on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still email us with any privacy request and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
