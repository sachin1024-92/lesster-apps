Privacy Policy: VaultMask: Private ID Vault

Android app package: app.vaultmask.privateid · Developer: Lesster

This policy explains what information the VaultMask: Private ID Vault app ("the app") handles, and how. In short: we do not collect your personal data on our servers.

Your documents stay on your device

VaultMask is designed to work fully offline. The ID documents and images you add (for example Aadhaar, PAN or other ID cards) are stored only on your device, encrypted inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or to any server, and we have no way to access them.

When you share a document, the app creates a masked copy (for example hiding the first 8 digits of an Aadhaar number) with an optional purpose stamp. The copy goes only to the app or person you pick in the Android share sheet. The share log that records what you shared, when and for what purpose is also stored only on your device.

Backups and deletion

Because nothing is stored on our servers, we cannot recover your vault if you lose your phone, forget your lock, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes the vault and the share log.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional): only if you choose to photograph a document inside the app.
  • Photos / files: through the Android system picker, limited to the files you select.
  • Biometric / device lock (optional): used by Android to unlock the vault. Your fingerprint or face data never reaches the app.
  • Internet: used only for Google Play Billing for the Pro upgrade. Your documents are never sent over the internet.

Advertising and analytics

This app does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the app can unlock Pro features. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. the app does not send your data to third parties.

Security

Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent over the internet (such as Google Play Billing) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is kept on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time from within the app where available, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of it on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still email us with any privacy request and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
