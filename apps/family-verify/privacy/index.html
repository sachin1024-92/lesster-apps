Privacy Policy: Family Verify

Android app package: app.familyverify · Developer: Lesster

This policy explains what information the Family Verify app ("the app") handles, and how. In short: we do not collect your personal data on our servers.

No personal data collected

Family Verify works entirely on your device. The safe word, family notes and settings you save are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We do not collect analytics, contacts or any personal information through this app.

Permissions

  • The app requests no phone permissions: no contacts, call log, SMS, microphone, camera or location access.
  • Internet: used only for Google Play Billing if you buy the Pro upgrade.

Family Verify provides general safety guidance and is not legal advice. In India, report cyber fraud by calling 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in. Police and government agencies never place anyone under "digital arrest" over a video call.

Advertising and analytics

This app does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the app can unlock Pro features. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. the app does not send your data to third parties.

Security

Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent over the internet (such as Google Play Billing) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is kept on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time from within the app where available, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of it on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still email us with any privacy request and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
