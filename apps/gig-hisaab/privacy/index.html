Privacy Policy: Gig Hisaab: Gig Earnings Tracker

Android app package: app.gighisaab.ledger · Developer: Lesster

This policy explains what information the Gig Hisaab: Gig Earnings Tracker app ("the app") handles, and how. In short: we do not collect your personal data on our servers. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, described below.

Your earnings data stays on your device

Gig Hisaab stores the trips, earnings, fuel and expense entries you add only on your device (in a local database). We do not run a server for this app, and your financial entries are not uploaded to us, shared or sold. The app does not connect to your delivery or ride-hailing accounts and does not read your SMS or bank messages.

Voice entry

If you use the optional Hindi or English voice entry, the app uses your phone's built-in Android speech recognition service to turn your speech into text. That service (for example Google's speech service) may send the microphone audio to Google, depending on your device settings, under its own privacy policy (the Google Privacy Policy). The app itself does not record, store or upload your audio; it only receives the resulting text.

Exports

If you export a report or backup, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Permissions

  • Microphone (optional): only when you tap the voice entry button. Android's speech recognizer may send that audio to Google.
  • Internet: used for the AdMob banner, Google Play Billing, and for online speech recognition if your device's speech service uses the network.
  • Advertising ID: requested so the app can show a Google AdMob banner.
  • No location, contacts, SMS or call log permissions are requested.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner. The app requests your device's advertising ID so ads can be served and measured. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade (product id: pro_yearly, a yearly subscription) is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the app can unlock Pro features. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. Apart from Google AdMob (for the banner), Google Play Billing (if you subscribe to Pro), and the Android speech recognizer (only if you use voice entry, which may send microphone audio to Google), the app does not send your earnings data to third parties. Your ledger stays on your device.

Security

Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent over the internet (such as ad requests, Google Play Billing, and any audio sent by the device speech service) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is kept on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time from within the app where available, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of it on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still email us with any privacy request and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
