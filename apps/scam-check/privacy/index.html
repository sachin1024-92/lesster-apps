Privacy Policy: Scam Check

Android app package: india.scamcheck · Developer: Lesster

This policy explains what information the Scam Check app ("the app") handles, and how. In short: we do not collect your personal data on our servers.

What the app does with your messages

Scam Check analyses only the text you paste into the app or share to it from another app. The analysis runs on your phone. The app does not ask for permission to read your SMS, call log or contacts, and it does not read any message you have not given it yourself.

We do not upload, store or sell the messages you check. Your check history, if the app keeps one, is stored only on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Optional link check (Google Safe Browsing)

If you turn on the optional online link check, web addresses (URLs) found in the message you are checking may be sent to Google's Safe Browsing service so it can tell the app whether the link is known to be dangerous. Only the link is used for this, not the rest of your message. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. If you leave the online check off, nothing leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only for the optional Safe Browsing link check and Google Play Billing.
  • No SMS, call log, contacts, location, camera or microphone permissions are requested.

Scam Check gives a risk estimate to help you decide. It cannot guarantee that a message is safe. If you have lost money to a cyber fraud in India, call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

Advertising and analytics

This app does not show ads and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the app can unlock Pro features. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. Apart from the Google services described above, the app does not send your data to third parties.

Security

Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent over the internet (such as Google Play Billing) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is kept on your device, you control it. You can delete it at any time from within the app where available, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy of it on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete; you can still email us with any privacy request and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
